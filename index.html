PlayShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

PlayShield is developed and maintained by the PlayShield team. For privacy questions, contact Kinhluanusa@gmail.com.

PlayShield: Touch Screen Blocker stores touch-blocking profiles and app settings locally on the device. PlayShield does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The app uses Android Accessibility Service access only to display and operate the user-configured touch-blocking overlay across other apps. This access is not used by PlayShield to read, upload, or transmit personal content.

When the live editor is used, screenshots are processed locally on the device to help the user draw blocked areas. PlayShield does not upload or retain those screenshots on a PlayShield server.

PlayShield displays Google Mobile Ads on the setup screen. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and personalize ads according to the user's consent and Google Play settings. Their handling of data is governed by Google's policies. PlayShield does not place ads over the touch-blocking overlay or media player.

PlayShield protects locally stored settings using Android app storage. Profiles and settings remain on the device until the user removes them, clears PlayShield app data, or uninstalls the app. PlayShield does not maintain a server-side account or retention of these local profiles. For support, contact Kinhluanusa@gmail.com.
