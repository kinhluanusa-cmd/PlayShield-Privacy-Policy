PlayShield Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

PlayShield: Touch Screen Blocker stores touch-blocking profiles and app settings locally on the device. PlayShield does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The app uses Android Accessibility Service access only to display and operate the user-configured touch-blocking overlay across other apps. This access is not used to read, upload, or transmit personal content. Live editor screenshots are processed locally and are not uploaded by PlayShield.

PlayShield displays Google Mobile Ads on the setup screen. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and personalize ads according to the user's consent and Google Play settings. PlayShield does not place ads over the touch-blocking overlay or media player. Users can remove locally stored profiles and settings by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
